# Platform fee shown before payment · #RL000006
> ⚙ #RL000006SGV1.0 · V1.0 in-progress

## Description
**R1 · EARS** When a price for specific dates is shown, the system shall show the platform fee as its own line and the total the User will pay, before any payment input.

## Why
Hidden fees at the last step are the most common complaint about marketplaces; a visible fee costs a little conversion and saves a lot of trust.
