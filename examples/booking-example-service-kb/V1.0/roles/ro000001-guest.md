# Guest · #RO000001
> ⚙ #RO000001SOV1.0 · V1.0 in-progress

## Description
Someone browsing without an account: searches by place and dates, compares listings, reads reviews. Often arrives from a search engine on the phone and decides in a few minutes whether to continue. Can do everything except book, message or review.

## Why
Most first visits come from people who have not decided yet; forcing sign-up before they see anything loses them.
