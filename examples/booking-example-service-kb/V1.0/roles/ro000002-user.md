# User · #RO000002
> ⚙ #RO000002SIV1.0 · V1.0 in-progress

## Description
A signed-in traveller: books and pays, keeps track of trips, cancels when plans change, messages the owner about a booking, writes reviews after a stay and manages the account. Travels a few times a year; wants certainty about dates, price and what happens if something goes wrong.

## Why
Booking money and messages need an identity on both sides; a signed-in account is the smallest thing that gives it.
