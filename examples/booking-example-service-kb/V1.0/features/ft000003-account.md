# Account · #FT000003
> ⚙ #FT000003SGV1.0 · V1.0 in-progress

## Description
Users need to manage who they are on the platform: profile, sign-in details, payment methods, notifications, and the step into hosting.
In scope: profile, email and password, payment methods, notification settings, the entry to Become an owner. Out of scope: deleting the account inside the app (handled by support in the first release).

## Why
Identity and payment details are reused across every booking, so they live once in the account.
