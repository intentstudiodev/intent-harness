# Hosting · #FT000004
> ⚙ #FT000004SGV1.0 · V1.0 in-progress

## Description
Owners need to bring a place onto the marketplace and run it: onboarding, listings, availability, requests and payouts.
In scope: owner onboarding with identity check, listings with photos and pricing, calendar, requests and bookings, payouts. Out of scope: multi-property teams, channel sync with other marketplaces, smart-lock integrations.

## Why
Without owners there is no supply; the owner side must be as polished as the guest side.

## ⚙ Sub-features
- [#FT000010 Become an owner](ft000010-become-an-owner.md) — Lets a User register as an Owner, including the identity check.
- [#FT000011 Manage listings](ft000011-manage-listings.md) — Lets an Owner create, edit, publish and unpublish listings: description, photos, price and house rules.
- [#FT000012 Calendar and availability](ft000012-calendar-and-availability.md) — Lets an Owner see bookings on a calendar and block or open dates.
- [#FT000013 Requests and bookings](ft000013-requests-and-bookings.md) — Lets an Owner review incoming booking requests, accept or decline them, and see confirmed and past bookings.
- [#FT000014 Payouts](ft000014-payouts.md) — Lets an Owner see upcoming and past payouts and where the money goes.
