# Find a stay · #FT000001
> ⚙ #FT000001SGV1.0 · V1.0 in-progress

## Description
Guests and users need to find a place that fits their dates, group and budget before anything else happens. This is the front door of the marketplace.
In scope: search by place, dates and guests; results with price and rating; filters. Out of scope: map-based exploring without dates, saved searches, wish lists.

## Why
Every booking starts with a search; if finding is slow or thin, nothing downstream matters.
