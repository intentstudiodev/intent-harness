# Trips · #FT000002
> ⚙ #FT000002SGV1.0 · V1.0 in-progress

## Description
Users need one place for everything that happens after they decide: paying, upcoming and past stays, cancelling, messaging the owner and reviewing.
In scope: booking and payment, the trip list and detail, cancellation, reviews, messages about a booking. Out of scope: travel insurance, flights, group payment splitting.

## Why
After payment the user's questions change from 'which place' to 'what now'; one home for those answers lowers support load. [assumed]

## ⚙ Sub-features
- [#FT000006 Book and pay](ft000006-book-and-pay.md) — Lets a User request a stay for chosen dates and pay for it in one pass.
- [#FT000007 Cancel a booking](ft000007-cancel-a-booking.md) — Lets a User cancel an upcoming trip and see exactly what is refunded.
- [#FT000008 Reviews](ft000008-reviews.md) — Lets a User rate and describe a completed stay, and shows those reviews on the listing.
- [#FT000009 Messages](ft000009-messages.md) — Lets a User and an Owner talk about a specific booking request or trip inside the product.
