# Payments provider · #TC000003
> ⚙ #TC000003SGV1.0 · V1.0 in-progress

## Description
An external payments service that holds and captures card payments, issues refunds and pays owners out to their bank accounts; the product never stores card numbers.

## Why
Holding and later capturing a payment, refunds and owner payouts are the hard parts of a marketplace; a provider that does all three keeps card data and compliance out of the product. ## Alternatives
- [~ Own card handling with a bank acquirer]
