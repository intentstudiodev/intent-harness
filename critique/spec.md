# Critique: a spec
- Could someone build from this alone? What would they have to guess?
- Is the why chain real (feature → user goal → purpose) or pasted?
- Do acceptance conditions follow from the why? Which contradict it?
- Are fixed and guidance kept apart, and are the marks justified by cards?
- Trade-offs and assumptions stated per block?
- Is the scope's edge drawn and said?
- Stale: did any source card change since made-from?
