# Contributing

- **Improvement notes**: the harness can send them for you (`self-improvement/share-note.md`), anonymised and shown to you first; or open an issue with the "Improvement note" template.
- **By pull request**: starters, connector registry entries (with licence and last-checked date), checklists, templates. These have a fixed shape and are cheap to review.
- **Through a discussion first**: roles, skills, the entry file, the philosophy. The philosophy changes only by the maintainer, after discussion.
- Contributions are accepted under the same licence as the harness (MIT).
- Anything in the public feedback can be removed on request.
