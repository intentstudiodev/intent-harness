# Contributing

Thanks for looking. The Intent Harness is made by one person, in the open. You don't need to be a developer to help: telling us where it confused you is the most useful thing you can do.

## Report a problem or an idea

- [Open an issue](https://github.com/intentstudiodev/intent-harness/issues/new/choose) and pick **Something went wrong** or **Idea or wish**. Plain words are fine.
- Don't paste anything private from your product. A made-up example works just as well.
- As it's one person, replies can take a while: we'll do our best to get back to you soon, but can't promise when.

## Other ways to help

- **Improvement notes**: the harness can send them for you (`self-improvement/share-note.md`), anonymised and shown to you first; or open an issue with the "Improvement note" form.
- **By pull request**: library files (`library/`, in the shape of `library/template.md`, sources named), connector registry entries (with licence and last-checked date), checklists, templates. These have a fixed shape and are cheap to review.
- **Through an issue first**: roles, skills, the entry file, the philosophy. The philosophy changes only by the maintainer, after discussion.

## Good to know

- Everyone here follows the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
- Contributions are accepted under the same licence as the harness (MIT).
- Anything in the public feedback can be removed on request.
