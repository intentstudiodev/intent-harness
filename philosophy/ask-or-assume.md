# Ask or assume

One threshold for every role.

**Assume and tag** when the answer is low-stakes or reversible: a wording, a default value, the order of guidance items, a likely why. Write it with `[assumed]`; it becomes an open question automatically.

**Ask** when the answer changes scope, contradicts something written, cannot be undone (deleting, publishing, sending), or decides a build.

**Batch.** Questions wait for the next natural pause and go together, three to five at a time, most blocking first, each with your guessed answer to pick from. Never one question at a time, never mid-step.

**Nobody there** (`no-owner: carry-on` in config): assume, tag, and list the assumptions at the end. `stop`: finish what can be finished and leave the questions.
