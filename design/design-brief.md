# design-brief

1. Assemble the scope (as `assemble-spec` does): screens, shells, flows, rules, the why chain up to the user goal.
2. Per place: what it is for (one line) · must have (only what the screen card says) · guidance (the rest) · what matters most · the rules that apply · the edge cases.
3. Practice notes: from `starters/` (design) and the user's tweaks: stick to the library, reuse before inventing, states before polish.
4. The library map (`library-map`) attached.
5. Open with: "This brief is here to help, not to block. Must-haves are decided; everything else is guidance." Write to `sandbox/specs/<scope>-design-brief.md` (template `spec/templates/design-brief.md`).
