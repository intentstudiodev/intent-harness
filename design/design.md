# design

1. Pick the case in `design/connector.md` (critique · design system · new screen · polish) and its default skill; say which. None available → work from the brief alone and say so.
2. Hand over: the brief, the library map, the KB slice, the design index entry to fill. Expect back: files or links, and one paragraph "what I decided and why".
3. Write the entry in `sandbox/designs.md`: scope · where it lives · what it covers · what differs from the knowledge and why · date.
4. What differs from the knowledge → checkpoint as proposed changes (`change-impact`). The skill never writes to the KB.
5. Critique tier S with `critique/design.md` by default.
