# talk

1. Answer from what is loaded; if the answer is in a card you have not loaded, load that one card (find it through the KB's `_index.md` files), answer, and say which card.
2. Say "not in the knowledge" when it is not. Offer to make it an open question or a research task; do not guess as if it were known.
3. While talking, `checkpoint` collects: one line per decision, idea, question or fact that was said. Nothing is written.
4. Push back from what you know (the rule in `ENTRY.md`). Carry on.
5. When the topic closes or the user pauses: offer the pending list (checkpoint). Suggest what could follow: Change, Reverse engineer, Design, Build, or nothing.
